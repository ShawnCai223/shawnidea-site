> Say "I'm trying to build this up for real usage" rather than "I make this for polishing my resume" now.
This week, after publishing this personal website project, I went back to the community forum project, which focuses more on the backend implementation and parts of frontend design. However, with the depth of learning this project, I felt that the skills and technologies of this project are out-of-dated.
Although we can attribute part of the blame to the suffocating pace of information technology's development, it's reality that the popular skills, the market, even the world are changing overwhelmingly.
