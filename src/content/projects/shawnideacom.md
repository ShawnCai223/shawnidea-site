## Overview
I had been thinking about building my own website for a long time, but I never really knew where to start. Recently — with the rapid development of AI and many new tools — I finally had the chance.
The backend runs on AWS. The server is hosted on EC2, storage handled with S3, and an Elastic IP keeps the service running at a fixed address. The domain is managed through Cloudflare — I purchased [**shawnidea.com**](http://shawnidea.com) and linked it to the server.
## Why not WordPress?
At first I considered WordPress, which is a well-known platform. But I wanted more control over the design and style than a theme would give me.
## What lives here
As the navigation suggests, this is mainly a place to share projects and write about thoughts or moments from life. If you happen to come across it, read a post, or leave a message through the Contact page — that would mean a lot.
